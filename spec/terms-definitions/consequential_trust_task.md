[[def: consequential Trust Task, consequential Trust Tasks]]

~ A *Trust Task* whose successful execution has an effect the *consumer* cannot silently undo: it alters *recipient party* state, releases confidential material, or exercises the subject's own authority. Precisely, a *Trust Task* is *consequential* where any of the following holds — `sideEffects.level` is `mutating` or `destructive`, `exposure.discloses` is `secret`, or `exposure.actsAsSubject` is `true` (see [Specification Requirements](#specification-requirements) items 13 and 14). Where a *consumer* determines the authoritative values from the handler it is about to invoke rather than from the values the *Trust Task specification* declares, the authoritative values govern. An absent, unrecognized, or unresolvable declaration is *consequential*, consistent with the fail-safe defaults of [Specification Requirements](#specification-requirements) items 13 and 14. The term names a class of task for which several rules in this specification are stricter than the general case; it carries no authorization meaning of its own (see [Consumer Requirements](#consumer-requirements) item 10).
