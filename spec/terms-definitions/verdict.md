[[def: verdict, verdicts]]

~ The result a *Trust Ceremony*'s policy returns at its verdict gate: exactly one of **allow** (the transition proceeds), **deny** (it is refused), **refer** (a human or quorum must decide) or **request more** (evidence the actor can supply might change the outcome). A deny takes one of two forms by its reason: `permissionDenied` where the requester may not ask, or the task's success response where a task that exists to produce a decision answers no. See [Verdicts and the Replies That Carry Them](#verdicts-and-the-replies-that-carry-them).
